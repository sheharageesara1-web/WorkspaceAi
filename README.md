StudySpace AI
StudySpace AI is an intelligent notes and learning management system built for students. It brings note-taking, AI-powered study help, group collaboration, and smart reminders together into one secure, easy-to-use web platform — accessible from any device.

🗂 Smart Notes Management
Students can create rich-text notes with formatting, organize them by subject and custom tags, and mark important notes as favorites. Documents such as PDF, DOCX, and TXT files can be uploaded and automatically converted into fully editable notes using built-in text extraction.

🤖 AI Study Assistant
An integrated AI tutor helps students study faster. It answers questions about their notes, generates concise summaries, and creates practice quiz questions for revision. The assistant works as a dedicated chat panel or a floating assistant available anywhere in the app, with fair daily usage limits.

👥 Study Groups & Real-Time Collaboration
Classmates can be invited into study groups, and notes can be shared with viewer or editor permissions. Shared notes support real-time collaborative editing, showing who is online and editing, with inline comments and full version history to track every change.

⏰ Smart Reminders & Notifications
The system automatically sends reminders for upcoming exams and tasks. Students can configure their own notification preferences, including quiet hours and timezone-aware scheduling, so they get alerts at the right time.

📊 Unified Dashboard
A single dashboard shows upcoming exams, tasks, recent notes, uploaded files, study groups, and AI conversations at a glance. The interface is fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with dark mode and adjustable font sizes for accessibility.

🔐 Secure & Private
Every student account is protected by full authentication, and row-level security ensures notes stay private and visible only to their authorized collaborators.
